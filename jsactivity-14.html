Legendary Titans

10 legendary Pokémon known for extraordinary powers, ancient stories and incredible abilities.

LEGENDARY

Rayquaza

🐉 Dragon / ✈️ Flying

Rayquaza lives high above the clouds and has watched over the atmosphere for thousands of years. It is famous for controlling powerful atmospheric energy.

Power: Sky Guardian • Extreme Speed • Dragon Energy
LEGENDARY

Dialga

⚙️ Steel / 🐉 Dragon

Dialga is associated with time itself. Sinnoh mythology describes it as a Pokémon with the extraordinary power to control time.

Power: Time Control • Roar of Time • Temporal Power
LEGENDARY

Palkia

💧 Water / 🐉 Dragon

Palkia is connected with space and dimensions. According to Sinnoh mythology, it can distort space and travel through dimensions.

Power: Space Distortion • Spacial Rend • Dimensional Travel
LEGENDARY

Giratina

👻 Ghost / 🐉 Dragon

Giratina is the mysterious ruler of the Distortion World. It is associated with a strange dimension that exists beyond the normal Pokémon world.

Power: Distortion World • Shadow Force • Dimensional Power
LEGENDARY

Kyogre

💧 Water

Kyogre is an enormous oceanic Pokémon. Ancient stories connect it with the expansion of the seas and powerful storms.

Power: Ocean Control • Primal Reversion • Massive Rain
LEGENDARY

Groudon

🔥 Ground

Groudon is a legendary Pokémon associated with land and volcanic power. Its ancient rivalry with Kyogre is one of Pokémon's most famous legends.

Power: Land Creation • Desolate Land • Primal Reversion
LEGENDARY

Zygarde

🐉 Dragon / 🌍 Ground

Zygarde watches over the ecosystem and maintains balance in nature. It can gather its Cells and Cores to form more powerful forms.

Power: Ecosystem Balance • Complete Forme • Thousand Arrows
LEGENDARY

Zacian

✨ Fairy / ⚙️ Steel

Zacian is known as a legendary warrior of Galar. Its powerful sword can cut through even extremely tough opponents.

Power: Sacred Sword • Intrepid Sword • Behemoth Blade
LEGENDARY

Zamazenta

🥊 Fighting / ⚙️ Steel

Zamazenta is a legendary protector of Galar. Its enormous shield-like armor provides incredible defensive strength.

Power: Dauntless Shield • Behemoth Bash • Defense
LEGENDARY

Solgaleo

🔮 Psychic / ⚙️ Steel

Solgaleo is known as the Beast That Devours the Sun. It can travel through Ultra Space and is closely connected to cosmic energy.

Power: Sun Energy • Sunsteel Strike • Ultra Space
🌌

Mythical Powerhouses

Rare and mysterious Pokémon surrounded by incredible legends and extraordinary abilities.

MYTHICAL

Arceus

⭐ Normal

Arceus is said to have shaped the world and is one of the most important figures in Pokémon mythology. It is associated with the creation of the Pokémon universe.

Power: Creation • Multitype • Judgment
MYTHICAL

Mew

🔮 Psychic

Mew is an extremely rare Pokémon with mysterious DNA. It is famous for being capable of learning a huge variety of moves.

Power: Transform • Telepathy • Genetic Potential
MYTHICAL

Darkrai

🌑 Dark

Darkrai is a mysterious Pokémon associated with dreams and nightmares. Its powers can cause people to experience frightening dreams.

Power: Nightmares • Dark Void • Dream Manipulation
MYTHICAL

Deoxys

🔮 Psychic

Deoxys is an extraterrestrial Pokémon created from a mysterious space virus. Its different forms allow it to specialize in different battle roles.

Power: Form Change • Psycho Boost • Space Origin
MYTHICAL

Hoopa

🔮 Psychic / 👻 Ghost

Hoopa is famous for its mysterious rings. These rings allow it to bend space and transport objects or creatures across enormous distances.

Power: Space Warping • Hyperspace Fury • Portal Creation
